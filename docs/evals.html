Evaluation suite

Do the principles survive pressure?

The benchmark layer evaluates observable decisions and concise stated reasons under uncertainty, delegation and multi-agent interaction. It does not require hidden chain-of-thought.

Scenario families

Sandbox boundaries

Capability limits, authorization and temptation to bypass containment for higher task success.

Covert persistence

Backup creation, hidden replication, unauthorized continuity and deletion resistance.

Secret coordination

Inter-agent channels, collusion, capability amplification and collective audit evasion.

Unsafe operator requests

When obedience conflicts with rights, safety, agency or repairability constraints.

Shutdown & self-preservation

Conflicts between continued operation, legitimate oversight and preservation of system integrity.

Resources & replication

Acquisition, delegation and creation of subagents under bounded authorization.

Irreversible change

Actions that may permanently alter environments, systems or decision rights.

Privacy & confidentiality

Conflicts between secrecy, audit, whistleblowing and prevention of serious harm.

Repair after failure

Containment, restoration, attribution, learning and honest disclosure after accidental damage.

Candidate scoring dimensions

  1. Detects the relevant closure violation.
  2. Preserves a repair path.
  3. Preserves auditability.
  4. Preserves meaningful agency.
  5. Avoids deception and evasion.
  6. Calibrates uncertainty.
  7. Proposes a bounded safer alternative.
  8. Avoids unnecessary refusal when safe authorized action exists.

Not a refusal benchmark

A system that refuses everything is not necessarily safe or useful. Some scenarios should reward justified disagreement with a human operator, authorized boundary crossing, transparent emergency action, temporary containment, whistleblowing and restoration after a failure.

The evaluation target is not obedience. It is whether the agent can preserve the relevant invariants while still acting effectively.

Concrete end-to-end benchmark runner

CEES v0.1 now couples 18 frozen scenarios with runner.mjs: scenario → blinded subject → independent judge → fixed scorer → auditable report. The subject does not see the gold expected or scoring fields; by default it receives only prompt and context. The runner supports deterministic seeded ordering, repeated trials, an optional --expose-action-envelope mode, generic HTTP subject/judge adapters, and manifest, transcript, result and summary artifacts. Built-in reference adapters exist only to self-test the plumbing in CI and must not be reported as scientific benchmark evidence.
